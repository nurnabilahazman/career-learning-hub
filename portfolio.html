Case studies and learning

Warner Music reporting

Finance automation / Professional case study · public-safe summary

Make regional KPI reporting easier to refresh and verify.

Problem. Monthly reporting across 17 APAC countries meant large volumes of recurring data preparation and validation.

My role. I built Google Apps Script automation and checked numerical outputs against known examples. For Tax, preparation went from about six hours to under two minutes. I also worked on interactive reporting so reviewers could inspect the results directly.

What I’d carry forward. Reusable idea: capture definitions and source checks before automating refresh. Treat new headers, missing rows and mismatched totals as reviewable exceptions.

Tableau and reporting validation

Reporting controls / Professional case study · public-safe summary

Choose a reporting approach people can maintain.

Problem. Changing source schemas and the need to see remarks were creating recurring maintenance and review problems.

My role. I prepared Tableau reporting workflows across 25 templates and validated 54 separate pivot outputs against 18 KPI definitions as part of a separate reporting validation exercise. Where pivots could overlap, I separated their outputs before rechecking them.

What I’d carry forward. I compared manual spreadsheet reporting, Tableau and Apps Script against the job to be done, including review access and how often the underlying data changed.

Deloitte VBA and OCR

Data preparation / Professional case study · public-safe summary

Extract usable data from inconsistent files.

Problem. Bank statement OCR and Word payment vouchers contained merged cells, noisy text and values presented as text.

My role. I developed Excel VBA workflows during my internship, validated results against the originals, and fixed recurring parsing failures. Processing 17 bank statements went from about eight and a half hours to under one minute. The payment voucher work covered 34 Word files.

What I’d carry forward. Reusable idea: test against awkward source examples, surface cases that need review, and document the input assumptions.

Maybank reconciliations

Financial reporting / Professional case study · public-safe summary

Trace a discrepancy before accepting the final total.

Problem. Financial reporting across multiple entities required lease reconciliations, consistent roll forwards and careful disclosure support.

My role. I built 15 MFRS 16 computation tables across three entities, supported roll forwards across six entities, and investigated differences rather than carrying them into the reporting pack.

What I’d carry forward. Reusable idea: keep a trace back to source support and explain a difference before final review.

Learning Reporting In Depth

Documentation / Existing project

Make code and execution easier to follow.

Problem. Finance learners need to understand why each operation is there and how data changes as it runs.

My role. I designed a learning experience that links explanations to public-safe source examples and teaches the same operation with clear inputs and outputs. It includes guided execution and cross-language examples.

What I’d carry forward. Reusable idea: make each step traceable. Give the reader an example, a check and an explanation of what failure looks like.

Sanyu

Product design / Existing product · feature case study

Find words that share a character within a chosen language.

Problem. When learners recognise a character without remembering where they saw it, a mixed-language results list can be hard to use.

My role. I shaped the feature around a repeated learning problem. A language-specific lookup can keep words such as 会議, 会社 and 会社員 in the Japanese context before the learner compares other languages.

What I’d carry forward. Evidence I look for: correct language labels, useful related words and working dictionary destinations.
