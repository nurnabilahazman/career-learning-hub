Important budgeting rules

01 · Skip fees

For JLPT you can sit N2 without previously paying for N5, N4 or N3. Learn their content first.

02 · TOPIK is different

TOPIK II is one exam that can award grades 3–6. You do not purchase four separate grade exams.

03 · Shared subscription

One CFI Self-Study subscription includes FPAP and BIDA, not a separate fee for each.

Malaysia language exam calendar

Save two dates for each exam: the registration date or deadline, and the test day. All times are Malaysia time. Dates checked 20 November 2026.

Japanese · JLPT

Check from 1 March for the July sitting. Check from 1 August for December. These are reminders to check, and the 2027 application windows still need confirmation.

In Malaysia, July registration opened 17 March in 2025 and 16 March in 2026. December registration opened 18 August in 2025 and 17 August in 2026. The 6 December 2026 sitting is already closed for applications.

Check Japan Foundation KL announcements · Apply on the JLSM portal

Korean · TOPIK I / II

Start checking in December for next year’s Malaysian schedule. Check weekly in January until the notice appears, then save the opening date of the sitting you want.

The Korean Education Center published its 2026 schedule on 2 January. For the 18 October 2026 paper exam, Malaysian registration ran 8–22 July, about three months ahead. A 2027 Malaysian registration window was still unverified at the last check.

Korean Education Center in Malaysia → Notice · 2026 Malaysia schedule

Mandarin · HSK / HSKK

Check about one month before your preferred sitting. Pick your centre and level first. The linked Malaysian form gives the deadline to use.

UTAR Sungai Long, HSK 4 + speaking: for the 9 January 2027 exam, apply by 27 December 2026. For the 14 March 2027 exam, apply by 1 March 2027. Both forms list HSK at 9 a.m. and HSK 4 speaking at 11:20 a.m. Arrive one hour early.
